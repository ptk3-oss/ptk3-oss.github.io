Here's my paper prototype, 

**Cat Nap Sleep Mask**

This sleep mask acts like a mini “sunrise” alarm clock. When the wearer presses the button, the mask begins a short nap timer. After 10, 30, or 60 minutes, it gently wakes the user up with a slow LED fade-in and a ramping buzzer pulse. A light sensor measures ambient brightness, so if the room is already bright, the mask only uses the buzzer. 

In the final implementation, there will be a layer of fabric that covers the light and buzzer so that they don't get affected by sweat or moisture from the wearer's face. I wasn't sure where to fit the battery, so that will have to be maneuvered when it's getting put together.
