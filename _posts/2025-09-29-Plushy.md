Here's my plushy. 

This project involved creating a paper prototype, an aligator clip prototype, and then a final sewn implementation of a light-up plushy that has 3 LEDs controlled by a slide switch and a button. 

If I could go back in time, I would have been more patient with my conductive thread. It was very fragile and would frequently knot or break if I wasn't paying close attention. 
I also tried to tack the components with a couple of stitches at first, but it honestly didn't make it any easier to secure them, and it was a waste of time for me. 
I was also in a rush and forgot to decorate the rest of the plushy before I had already started my whip stitch, so I never got to implement my cool robot design that I wanted to do.
