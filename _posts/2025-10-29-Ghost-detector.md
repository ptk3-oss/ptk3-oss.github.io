Here's my Ghost Detector,

My Ghost Detector uses a light sensor and temperature sensor to decide when to turn on the onboard, and sewable red LEDs. The board lights up only when it’s both dark and cold.

A tip for my past self would be to look at the slides to figure out how to convert the raw temperature input into Celsius. I put it off to the end because I wanted to have the logic done, but there was a slide with exactly what I needed.
