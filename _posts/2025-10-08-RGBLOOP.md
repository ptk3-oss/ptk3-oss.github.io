Here's my RGB-Loop post, 

This program simulates cycling through a set of red, green, and blue LED's using loops and conditionals. The program counts down from a defined number of iterations called runTimes, and prints a color every 10th iteration. Each color is printed three times before switching to the next, controlled by a variable called colorCycles, which creates a repeating pattern. 

A tip for my past self would be to not overcomplicate the logic. The looping and printing weren't too hard to wrap my head around, but figuring out how I could cycle through the colors using the modulo and another variable took a lot of time.
