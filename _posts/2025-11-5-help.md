Here's my Help assignment, 

In this assignment, I created functions for dot, dash, my name in Morse code, and SOS in Morse code. When an alligator clip circuit is open, the buzzer plays my name in Morse code; when the circuit is closed, it plays my SOS in Morse code.

I originally tried to digitalWrite to the buzzer to play a sound, which kinda worked, but not really. So my tip for myself would be to use the tone() function because I could set it to play a specific frequency.
