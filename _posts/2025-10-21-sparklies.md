Here’s my Sparklies Project 


**Project Summary**  
All three programs rely on state-based logic and modular timing rather than using sequential delays. This approach preserves the ability to be able to have all go simultaneously in the future. 

**Tip for my past self:**  
Next time, I would buy an adapter for the USB cord, because I was doing a lot of the coding blind, which was fine for the flashbulb but impossible for the synchronous. The state-based approach was also really difficult for me; it's super unintuitive and just didn't make sense in my mind, and I really struggled, especially with the synchronous firefly. Seeing the example in class on Tuesday was very helpful for me, because the logic was fairly similar. 

---

### **Flash Bulb Firefly**
![Flash Bulb](/assets/img/midsem/flash.jpeg)  
This program flashes an LED for half a second every four seconds. 

---

### **Big Dipper Firefly**
![Big Dipper](/assets/img/midsem/dip.jpeg)  
This program gradually increases in brightness for about one second, stepping up the LED brightness by 30 every 100 milliseconds until it reaches full brightness, then turns off for the remainder of a 3-second cycle.

---

### **Synchronous Firefly**
![Synchronous](/assets/img/midsem/sync.jpeg)  
This program performs three quick flashes every ten seconds, turning on for 200 ms and off for 100 ms in each burst before remaining off for the rest of the cycle.
